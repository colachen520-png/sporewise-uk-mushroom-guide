How to use it

01 · Look

Take the cap, underside, stem base and habitat. One top-down photo is rarely enough.

02 · Compare

Check the likely species against its lookalikes. Missing a detail is a reason to stop.

03 · Confirm

Use a local fungus expert or society before eating. “Probably” is not a safety category.

Sporewise is an educational prototype. The database combines UK field sources and published toxicology, but it is not a medical service, foraging certification or substitute for an expert.

Evidence and image sources: Natural History Museum UK species · Fungi of Great Britain and Ireland · peer-reviewed mushroom-poisoning review · UK poison-centre study · photos via Wikimedia Commons.
